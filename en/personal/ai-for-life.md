# AI for personal life, not just work

> Live Practicum page. **Verified: June 2026.** The book focuses on work and the value you create, but AI can also help in your personal life. This is a curated set of use cases, not a random collection. Use it for direction and check current details before you act. This page is not financial, medical, or legal advice. Talk to a qualified professional when the stakes are high.

Use the same discipline you use at work. Do not send sensitive data to outside services. Do not trust an answer until you have checked it. Start with one use case.

---

## Personal finance and taxes

- Ask AI to explain a confusing statement or contract in plain English, then draft a list of questions for your bank.
- Sketch a budget or compare plans. Check every number yourself, and verify tax rules and deductions against a primary source.

## Learning and languages

- Use AI as a language tutor: "Guide me with questions. Do not give me the answer right away."
- Ask for an explanation at different levels, or make flashcards for review.
- **[NotebookLM](https://cheap-intelligence.vercel.app/en/watch/notebooklm)** lets you upload materials or videos and turn them into a course, study notes, or an audio overview. You can use it to prepare for exams or work through sources in another language, with explanations in your own language.

## Health admin, not diagnosis

- Prepare questions for your doctor or unpack unfamiliar terms in a medical record. Do not use the model for diagnosis or treatment. Take those questions to a doctor.

## Home and planning

- Plan a trip, build a shopping list from a recipe, make sense of instructions, or compare options.
- Use a personal agent for schedules and reminders. See [Agents available now](https://cheap-intelligence.vercel.app/en/watch/agents-now).

## Parenting from a personal angle

- Come up with activities for children or explain a difficult idea in language a child can follow.

## AI companions: benefits, limits, and safety

Companion apps are a separate, fast-growing category. They may act as a virtual conversation partner, a "friend," or an empathetic assistant. Under the hood, they are chat systems tuned to create emotional closeness. Mainstream assistants have started adding empathetic or supportive modes too. Check the current product name and format before you use one.

**Where they can help.** A companion may ease loneliness for a while, give you space to say your thoughts out loud, help you rehearse an important conversation, or offer support in the moment. That benefit can be real. It does not deserve ridicule.

**Where to draw the line.** The more an app tries to keep you engaged, the easier it is for the app to replace human contact instead of supporting it. Here the book's main idea works in reverse: **cheap synthetic closeness makes genuine connection more valuable**. A live conversation, someone's presence, and proof that "I am a real person" all become worth more, not less. A companion is a tool. It is not a relationship.

**Safety, especially for teenagers.** These are warning signs tied to specific dates, not a reason to panic. You can find the sources below.

- Common Sense Media reported in 2025 that about **72%** of US teenagers had tried an AI companion and about **52%** used one regularly.
- Regulators and courts have already stepped in. Italy's Garante fined Replika **€5 million** in 2025. In January 2026, Character.AI and Google agreed in principle to **settle** lawsuits concerning harm to teenagers, including the Sewell Setzer III case.

The answer is not simply "ban it." Personal AI also needs governance, meaning clear rules and human oversight. For a teenager, use age settings and talk about what the app does instead of relying on a ban. Everyone should keep sensitive information out of these systems. The privacy rule matters even more here. Emotional dependence on a model can create [cognitive debt](https://cheap-intelligence.vercel.app/en/watch/cognitive-practices), just as intellectual dependence can. Chapter 7, the authenticity thread, explains why real and provable human presence becomes more valuable.

> This page is not medical or psychological advice. If you are dealing with anxiety, depression, or a crisis, contact a qualified human professional rather than an AI companion. **Verified: June 2026. App details change quickly.**
