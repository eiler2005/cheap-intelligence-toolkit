# Trust Compass: two territories, two sides, one move

> Practicum for **Book 3, *The Economics of Trust*** (the Compass house, Chapter 13).
> Time: 40 to 60 minutes. The compass does not promise to detect a fake. It helps you choose the depth of verification in advance, name its limits, and avoid checking everything.

The compass has four parts:

- **needle:** the cost of an error;
- **depth:** from no verification to independent confirmation;
- **two sides:** how I verify others and what I show when others verify me;
- **four norms:** shared rules that nobody has to invent under pressure.

---

## 1. Set the needle before you read the message

Start with one question: **What will I lose if I am wrong?** Do not judge how convincing the message looks. The other party controls the presentation. You control the cost of an error.

| Depth | When to choose it | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **No questions** | The error is small and reversible, such as a video, image, or someone's opinion | Do not verify it. Save your attention for expensive decisions |
| **One question** | You plan to cite, forward, or base an opinion on it | Ask, "Where did this come from?" Find the source |
| **All three plus independent support** | Money, access, documents, health, work, or reputation | Check the origin, speaker, and owner of responsibility. Move to a second channel that the sender does not control |

## 2. Home territory

| How I verify | What I show when someone verifies me |
|---|---|
| A family check phrase stops a request. It does not authorize a payment | My family knows one stable number or channel where they can reach me |
| Every request for money ends with a call I make to a saved number | I have already said, "The real me will not be offended by a check" |
| Urgency triggers a pause until independent confirmation | Children and older relatives follow the same agreement and have permission to end the call |
| An agent that can spend money has a written limit and action list | I can show my authority and the assistant's limits instead of trying to remember them after an error |

|---|---|

## 5. Four points of the compass

A norm works when people say it aloud and apply it to everyone.

1. **Every family has its own phrase.**
   "If I call and ask for money, ask for the phrase. The real me will not be offended."
2. **Expensive actions require a second channel.**
   "We never transfer money because of one message. This rule applies to everyone, including me."
3. **A bot always identifies itself, and a person owns its words.**
   "A client must know whom they are speaking with. Every bot has a named person responsible for it."
4. **A detector does not pass sentence.**
   "A risk score from software gives us a reason to ask a question. It is not a basis for a decision about a person."

## 6. Who else reads your trail?

Some of the parties checking you are already software systems. A responsible person's name works better than "the team." A date works better than "recently." A number needs a unit. A research claim needs a primary source.

- [ ] My important name, role, and specialty are stated consistently on the main pages.
- [ ] My case studies include dates, source figures, and a clear result.
- [ ] My main claims link to primary sources.
- [ ] I document errors and corrections instead of hiding them.

Compare two AI answers about you. A difference is not a verdict. It points to a missing or unstable source. For current context, see [the machine web](https://cheap-intelligence.vercel.app/en/watch/machine-web).

## The first ninety minutes

1. **30 minutes:** List the three places where a mistake would cost you most.
2. **30 minutes:** Create independent support for the most expensive scenario. Record whom you call, which saved number you use, what you ask, and what you do if nobody answers.
3. **30 minutes:** State one norm to your family or team.
4. **Two more minutes:** See what one person and two different AI assistants can find about you.

> The useful result is not "I verify more." It is **"I verify somewhere else."**
> The compass makes deception more expensive. It does not make anyone invulnerable.
