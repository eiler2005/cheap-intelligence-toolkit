# Integrated profession audit map

> Practicum for Chapter 9, "Your Personal Career Audit." Time: 30 to 45 minutes.
> Use your real working week, not your resume or job description.

Chapter 9 does not add another tool. It **brings several earlier views into one picture**: your usefulness level from Chapter 5, personal harness from Chapter 6, AI working mode from Chapter 8, and Task-to-Outcome Map from Chapter 6. This worksheet gathers those results. If you already completed the other worksheets, copy their results here and update them.

Goal: Put your diagnosis on one page. Show what is getting cheaper, where the weak point sits, which part of your value can survive change, and where you can move up one level. There is no score or "replacement index." This is a map, not a personality test.
