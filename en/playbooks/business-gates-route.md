# Business route through the gates

> Practicum for **Volume 2, Chapter 11**, "The Change Compass" (business owner conclusion).
> Time: 30-40 minutes. This covers a **business and one function**, not a personal skill from Volume 1
> or a team process in the [Manager Route](manager-gates-route.md).

Do not try to "digitize the whole business." Put **one function** on supervised agents, starting with a pain point tied to money, and make it resilient.
Move by gates rather than dates with the [Order of Moves](change-gates.md). Building is cheap; delivery is expensive. Distribution and trust are the bottleneck.
