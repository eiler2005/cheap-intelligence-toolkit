# Audit your way of working with AI

> Practicum for Chapter 8, "Cognitive Debt." Time: 5 to 7 minutes.
> Use a real working week. The mode describes a task, not you as a person.

There are three modes:

- **Cyborg:** You work with the model and keep challenging its choices.
- **Centaur:** You divide the work in advance. Some parts go to the machine, and some stay with you.
- **Self-automator:** You hand over the entire task and leave the decision loop.

The goal is to find where you left the loop and step back into it.
