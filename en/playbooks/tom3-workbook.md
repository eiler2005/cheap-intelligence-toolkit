# Book 3 Workbook: The Economics of Trust

> The end-to-end Practicum route for *The Economics of Trust* (Book 3 in the
> *When Intelligence Became Cheap* series).
> This route through the contents asks **whom to trust when content, voices, and reputations are cheap
> to fake, and how to become someone people can trust without excessive checking.**
>
> **Verifiability** is the axis of this route. Do not rely on a verbal claim. Arrange your work so that
> important facts about you can be confirmed without your help. For the professional route, use the
> [Volume 1 Workbook](tom1-workbook.md). For the business route, use the
> [Volume 2 Workbook](tom2-workbook.md).

Trust has two sides in this volume. The first is **protection:** avoid a fake call, email, or review,
without paying the cost of checking everything. The second is **creation:** build a verifiable trail
so that people and even AI search can choose you with less doubt. The workbook covers both sides.

> **Did you arrive from a specific chapter?** The Practicum uses three "homes," not one page per
> chapter. The [chapter index](tom3-chapter-index.md) gives each chapter one row and links straight to
> its material.

## Minimum trust package

If you do not have time for the whole volume, complete these five worksheets in one evening:

1. [Family trust protocol](family-trust-protocol.md): agree on a check phrase, talk with parents and
   children, print a note, and decide what to do first after a scam. Think of it as a seat belt. Set it
   up before anything happens.
2. [Personal trust perimeter](trust-perimeter.md): cover the work side with a second channel for costly
   instructions, a saved trail of work, and protection if someone makes an accusation.
3. [The first 24 hours with an important file](chain-of-custody.md): preserve the original, action log,
   independent trace, and context before any of them disappear.
4. [Trust footprint map](trust-footprint-map.md): check what other people can already verify about you
   without your help and where the gaps are. Then choose one move for the next month.
5. [Honest AI policy](honest-ai-policy.md): give clients one page that says where AI works, where a
   human is guaranteed, and who owns an error.

These five worksheets cover the main route through the volume. You have basic protection, the
important file remains intact, and other people can verify and choose you more easily.

Complete this page after the minimum package, or start here if you need one result instead of ten
notes. Do not search for absolute authenticity. Decide in advance **what level of checking is enough
for the cost of this error.**

| Write this down | Home | Work and business |
|---|---|---|
| Costly situation | Who may ask for money, access, or an urgent decision? | Which instruction, payment, access request, or reputation risk must never be accepted from one message? |
| Second channel | Which saved number or personal channel will you use to check the request? | Who confirms the instruction outside the channel controlled by the sender? |
| Independent support | Who else can confirm the person or fact? | Where is the original, log, agreement, version, or other independent trace? |
| What I show | How do people close to you know how to check that it is really you? | How can a client or coworker see the decision owner, the limits on AI, and the work history? |
| Human owner | Who has the right to stop the payment or conversation? | Who owns the decision and repairs an error? |

## The volume's three homes

### Home 1: Protection (Chapters 1 to 5). Avoid traps without paying to check everything

The diagnosis is simple: convincing no longer means authentic. Voices, faces, reviews, and resumes
can all be faked. You do not need to check everything. Check the cases where an error is costly.

- [Family trust protocol](family-trust-protocol.md) for **home:** a check phrase, a conversation with
  parents and children, common schemes, limits for your own agents, a note by the phone, and the first
  hours after a scam.
- [Personal trust perimeter](trust-perimeter.md) for **work and money:** a second channel for costly
  instructions, a work trail created before an accusation, protection during an accusation or a
  review attack, and your personal "distrust tax" account.
- Live page, for readers in Russia: [protections available there](https://cheap-intelligence.vercel.app/en/watch/ru-fraud-protection), including voluntary
  credit bans, the "second hand" rule, cooling-off periods, and the rule that a bank does not write
  through messengers. These measures are free and take minutes to enable.

### Home 2: Verifiability and creation (Chapters 6 to 12). Keep a trail and become easier to verify

Procedure connects protection and trust. Preserve an important file and build your own trail over
time. When fakes are cheap, **verifiable human work** becomes more valuable.

- [Chain of custody: the first 24 hours](chain-of-custody.md) explains what to do with an important
  file before an expert review or dispute: preserve the original, a log, a checksum, and an independent trace.
- [Evidence package before a dispute](evidence-package.md) gives you a template for money, work, a
  deal, or reputation: what happened, where the original is, what the context was, and who can confirm
  it independently. It also asks how much checking is enough for a video, a hire, and a payment.
- Live page: [trust and regulation](https://cheap-intelligence.vercel.app/en/watch/trust-regulation) covers who accepts a digital file,
  how to check the origin of someone else's file, and who owns an agent's error.
- [Trust footprint map](trust-footprint-map.md) is the **flagship worksheet.** It puts three anchors,
  a verifiable history, live references, and a signed channel, across four channels: social media or
  Telegram, talks or conferences, books or publications, and community. Its main question is:
  *which parts are expensive to fake?*
- [Honest AI policy](honest-ai-policy.md) treats trust as part of the product. It asks you to state
  how AI is used and make clear who your offer is not for.
- [Trust inside a team](trust-inside-team.md) covers work controls: a register of bots that speak in
  your name and a hiring protocol for a world where an on-screen candidate is cheap to fake.
- [Trust repair protocol](trust-repair-protocol.md) covers the other side of a verifiable trail: what
  to do when you made the mistake. First decide whether competence or honesty failed, then use the
  right process for that failure.
- Live and reusable: the [AI search visibility kit](geo-ai-search-kit.md) helps neural systems find
  and cite you. Its trust layer asks not only whether you are visible, but what those systems say about you.

### Home 3: Compass (Chapter 13). Choose the depth and check both sides

The final chapter turns the material into a decision route. It relies on boring protocols that still
work when you are tired, not heroic vigilance.

- [Trust Compass](trust-compass.md) has one cost-of-error arrow, three levels of depth, two territories
  (Home and Work and business), two sides (how I check and what I show), and four norms.
- [Family trust protocol](family-trust-protocol.md) is a standalone route for a broad audience. It
  covers family members, parents, children, your own agents, and the first hours after a scam.
- Live page: the [regulatory tracker](https://cheap-intelligence.vercel.app/en/watch/trust-regulation) covers AI labeling, provenance,
  and laws. It changes quickly.

The volume is framed by **three trust questions:** "Who is speaking? What do they answer for? Who can
confirm it independently?" It also uses one rule: check what is costly, not what looks strange. The
book explains the questions, and the [Trust Compass](trust-compass.md) turns them into levels of review.
