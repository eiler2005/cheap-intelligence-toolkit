# Five scarcity audit

> Practicum for Chapter 7, "Skills That Become More Valuable." Time: 25 to 30 minutes.
> Use your last working week, not your resume.

**Faster option:** The [interactive browser version](https://cheap-intelligence.vercel.app/en/skills/five-deficits-audit/) helps you choose one scarce capability for the quarter and suggests a move. You do not need to install anything, and your data stays on your device.

As output gets cheaper, five capabilities become scarce: **judgment, taste, trust, attention, and distribution**. Here, distribution means getting your work in front of the right people. Authenticity is not a sixth item. It is a quality of all five. Each one becomes more valuable when people can see the human behind it.

This worksheet is not a list of "future skills." It is a diagnosis. Which scarce capability do you already have but the market cannot see? Which one is weak?

## Step 2. Five questions for each task

Answer one question about each scarce capability for every task.

| Scarce capability | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Judgment** | Which decisions depend on the situation and stay with me? What happens if the model makes them? |
| **Taste** | How many options do I choose from, and what standard tells me that one is good? |
| **Trust** | Does someone check my work again, or do they approve it without checking? |
| **Attention** | Did the result reach the person who makes the decision? |
| **Distribution** | Will anyone outside this task know that I did the work? |

## Check for self-deception

- A capability counts as strong only when you can name an example and its consequences. A self-rating is not enough.
- Other people's behavior confirms trust. For example, they approve your work without checking it again. Your own feeling does not confirm it.
- Make one move, not three. Splitting your effort across all five usually produces no change.
