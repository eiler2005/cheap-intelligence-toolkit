# Worksheet: my usefulness level

> Practicum for Chapter 5, "Five Levels of New Usefulness."
> Time: 30 to 40 minutes.
> Use your last working week, not your resume or job description.

**Faster option:** The [interactive browser version](https://cheap-intelligence.vercel.app/en/skills/usefulness-level-check/) identifies the level of a task and challenges inflated ratings. You do not need to install anything, and your data stays on your device.

Goal: Find the level of one specific task. This is not a rating of you as a person. It shows what you are paid for in the connection between your work, AI, and the result.

## Check for self-deception

- Producing more of the same output does not move you above level 1.
- If the model does everything and you only forward its answer, you are still at level 0, only faster.
- Without criteria for what "correct" means, you are not yet a validator.
- If nobody knows who owns the outcome, you are not yet the outcome owner.
- If the solution cannot run without you, it is not yet a system.
