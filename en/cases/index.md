# Worked examples

> See what the Practicum workbooks and worksheets look like after a real person fills them in.

A blank worksheet is honest but silent. It does not show how much detail a useful answer needs.
This section has three end-to-end examples written for composite personas, plus one review of a
public company.

!!! info "Examples, not answers to copy"
    The personas are composites, and their numbers are illustrative. The examples show the **depth
    and honesty of a useful answer**. They do not give you the "right answer." Yours will be different.

- **[Marina, a marketer](persona-marketolog.md)** works through all 10 steps of the
  [Volume 1 Workbook](../playbooks/tom1-workbook.md), from her value lever to a 90-day plan.
- **[Sergey, a support manager](persona-rukovoditel-podderzhki.md)** takes his function through the
  [Volume 2 Workbook](../playbooks/tom2-workbook.md): maturity, the first agent, a contract, and gates.
- **[Olga, the owner of a small online store](persona-vladelec-msp.md)** follows the small-business
  route: an agent-based staff map, the function with the fastest cash impact, and visibility to a
  customer agent.
- **[Marina and her family: a first trust protocol](persona-family-trust.md)** covers two costly
  Volume 3 situations: an urgent call from a relative and a client's request to change payment details.
- **[IKEA through the Change Compass](../playbooks/ikea-compass-example.md)** applies the four arrows
  and seven gates to a public case.
- **[Klarna: the win and the skipped gate](../playbooks/klarna-compass-example.md)** is a public case
  that shows where the agent paid off and which gate the company skipped.
