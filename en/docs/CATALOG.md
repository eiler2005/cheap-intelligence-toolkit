# Catalogue of worksheets

Everything in [`playbooks/`](../playbooks/), with what each one is for, grouped by book. Useful both
to a person looking for the right sheet and to an assistant handed this page: it sees what is
available and can suggest the right thing without following links.

A worksheet is a page with steps and blanks that you fill in yourself, on paper or in your notes.
You do not have to read the books first — each sheet explains its own model.

Nearby: [the skill instructions in full](../skills/) · [book packs](../../packs/en/) ·
the chapter-to-material map in [`MAP.md`](MAP.md).
