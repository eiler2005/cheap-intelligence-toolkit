# Role catalog: choose yours

> Entry point 1 in the Practicum: "How does AI change my work?" The roles are grouped by field. Each role page is a hub: task baskets, before and after, red flags, a minimum tool set, then a route into an audit and a plan. **Date: 2026-06.** If your exact role is not here, search by task in [_by-task.md](_by-task.md).

The full role-page template is [lawyer.md](lawyer.md). The other pages follow it.

You can also browse by the [company model](_company-model.md), based on Porter's value chain. For the wider picture, see the [data review](_super-analysis.md): what AI may replace and what remains, using GDPval, the Anthropic Economic Index, and O*NET.
