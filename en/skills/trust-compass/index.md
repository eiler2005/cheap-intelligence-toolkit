# Trust Compass: fill it in and save it

> A local-first tool for **Book 3, *The Economics of Trust*, Chapter 13**.
> You fill in two costly situations, a second channel, independent support, and one move. Your data
> stays in the browser and is never sent anywhere.

<a class="md-button md-button--primary" href="tool.html">Open Trust Compass →</a>

## What you will have

- one protocol for home and work;
- a Markdown version for your notes or a discussion with family and coworkers;
- a printable version;
- a short entry in [My Profile](../my-profile/index.md).

The tool does not detect fakes or decide whether a person, voice, image, or file is authentic. It helps
you name **which check you will treat as sufficient in advance** when the cost of an error is high.

Without JavaScript, use the [one-page result](../../playbooks/tom3-workbook.md#my-trust-protocol-on-one-page).
