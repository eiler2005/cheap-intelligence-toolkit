# My adaptation profile

> A local-first Practicum tool that shows completed diagnostics, your stage on the
> [maturity route](../../tracks/index.md), one next step, and a separate trust layer.

<a class="md-button md-button--primary" href="tool.html">Open My Profile →</a>

## What it is

After it calculates a result, each browser tool in the Practicum offers a **"Save to My Profile"**
button. The profile brings those results together:

- a table of the tools you completed, when you completed them, and what result you got;
- your current route stage, from 1 "understand your work" to 2 "build a system" to 3 "teams and
  processes," plus **one next step**, which is the first incomplete tool in that stage;
- a separate **trust layer** that shows whether you built a Trust Compass and when it needs another
  review. It does not change your maturity stage;
- a **Markdown export** for your notes or your AI assistant;
- a **reset** button that deletes the profile in one click.

## Privacy in plain language

- The profile stays **in your browser** through localStorage and **is never sent anywhere**, including
  to the author or a third party. The profile has no server.
- A different browser or device has a different, empty profile. Use the Markdown export to move it.
- Clearing this site's browser data deletes the profile. The "Reset" button does the same thing at once.
- The code is open under the MIT license. It is one readable file and makes no external requests.

## Without JavaScript

The profile is optional. The full Practicum still works without it. Keep your results in notes using
the "My one-page result" section of the [Volume 1 Workbook](../../playbooks/tom1-workbook.md) and the
[Volume 2 Workbook](../../playbooks/tom2-workbook.md). For costly decisions, use the
[Volume 3 Workbook](../../playbooks/tom3-workbook.md).
