# Practicum skills

The Practicum has two kinds of skills based on Volumes 1 and 2. All are free. Code uses the MIT
license, and text uses CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Every prompt skill below puts its full instructions in one code block. Use the copy button in the
corner and paste the entire block into any AI chat, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another
assistant available to you. Every browser tool also includes a chat version in case you cannot open the tool.
Look for the "Chat version if you cannot use the browser tool" section on its page.

an exercise from the book. You can collect results in [My Profile](my-profile/index.md), including
completed work, your [maturity route](../tracks/index.md) stage, and the next step. This data also stays
in your browser.

## Prompt skills for an AI assistant

These are complete instructions that you give to an AI assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT.

A `SKILL.md` file is an instruction your agent picks up on its own when a task falls into its area.
Pasting the text into a chat works too: each one also functions as an ordinary prompt. To write your
own, see [Turn a habit into a skill](../playbooks/make-it-a-skill.md).

- [Optimize My Work by Profession](optimize-my-work/SKILL.md) helps you break work into tasks and
  decide what AI can handle, what you should keep, and which tools may help.
- [Week inventory](week-inventory/SKILL.md) sorts your week into the 🟢/🟡/🔴 baskets and measures
  your exposure. Chapter 3.
- [The three value levers](value-levers-filter/SKILL.md) takes one task and says whether it is
  output or outcome, then gives one move. Chapter 2.
- [The five deficits audit](five-deficits-audit/SKILL.md) covers judgment, taste, trust, attention
  and distribution, and picks one deficit for the quarter. Chapter 7.
- [The agent contract](agent-contract/SKILL.md) fills ten fields and sets the threshold at which the
  agent stops itself. Book 2, chapter 3.
- [The trust compass](trust-compass/SKILL.md) sets the depth of checking by the cost of being wrong
  rather than by how convincing something looks. Book 3, chapter 13.
- [Explore Your Profession](../prompts/explore-my-profession.md) is a prompt for your own analysis.

Every skill for a book also travels inside its [single-file pack](../docs/HOW-TO-USE.md).
